CFI-Fed Helm chart (research prototype)

1. Build the image:
   docker build -t {{ .Values.image.repository }}:{{ .Values.image.tag }} .

2. Registry:
   kubectl -n {{ .Values.namespace }} port-forward svc/cfi-registry {{ .Values.registry.port }}:{{ .Values.registry.port }}

3. Signed audit export:
   curl http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.registry.port }}/audit/export/signed

Honesty: manifests demonstrate deployability only; not a hardened production deployment.
